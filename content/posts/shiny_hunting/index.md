# Shiny Hunting
What even is Shiny Hunting? You ask my dear friend? 
Well in the simplest way I can explain it is! Shiny hunting is a thing people do in Pokemon Video Games to get **Shiny Pokemon** which are rare alternate-colored variants distinguished by unique sparkle effects and sounds upon encounter. They were introduced in Gen II with Pokemon Gold, Sliver and Crystal. 

Functionally they are no different from a regular variants. They are only different in color. The base odd for a shiny is 1 in 8,192 in the earlier generatoins and 1 in 4,096 in newer generations.
