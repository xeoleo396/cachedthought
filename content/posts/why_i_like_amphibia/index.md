Anyways what even is Amphibia?!?

> Amphibia is an American animated television series created by Matt Braly. It premiered on June 14, 2019 on DisneyNOW and YouTube and on June 17, 2019 on Disney Channel.

And its premise is

> Amphibia chronicles the adventures of 13-year-old Anne Boonchuy, who is magically transported to the fantastical world of Amphibia, a rural marshland full of frog-people. There she meets an excitable young frog named Sprig Plantar, his unpredictable sister Polly and overprotective grandpa Hop Pop. With Sprig's help, Anne will transform into a hero and discover the first true friendship of her life.

So! How much did I enjoy it? Alot like Alot alot! I loved this show on my first watch and I loved watching it through second watch through as well. In all honesty the show was a wonderful. The story though basic is well executed while discussing themes of uphevel, toxic friendships, being yourself, and many more! I loved watching the characters grow. If I had to select my favorite characters in the series though for me most of the time its hard to have a favorite character since I love most of them but.. If I had to have just two then:
- Marcy Wu
- Hop Pop

Now I love the both of them! They are my favorites out of the entire show. Hop Pop has some of the most growth I have seen in a characters as well as some very very very funny moments. Marcy Wu due to the interest of keeping this review spoiler-free I will not be discussing her as much but my connection with her comes from the place of being the kid that is into nerdy stuff and his friends don't really enjoy it as much. I understand Marcy on some level that it hurts when someone you care about be it your siblings, parents, or friends... Do not really give a damn about what you have to say. I understand and relate to that feeling.
